03 / Commercial Terms

Simple, standard commercial terms.

No charge to candidates, ever. Our fee is a placement commission paid by the hiring company only after a successful candidate joining.

STANDARD COMMISSION RATE
8.33% to 25% of Annual CTC

Typically 8.33% (one month's salary) for junior to mid-level roles, and 15% to 25% for senior / executive roles.

PAYMENT TERMS
30 to 45 Days After Joining

Invoice is raised once the candidate joins, payable within 30 to 45 days of the candidate's joining date.

REPLACEMENT GUARANTEE
60 to 90-Day Free Replacement

If the placed candidate leaves or is terminated within 60 to 90 days of joining, we provide a free replacement for that position.

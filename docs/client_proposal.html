2. Step-by-Step Business Workflow

1

Job Requirement Posting & Founder Approval

Client HR submits a vacancy → System holds it in "Pending Approval" → Founders set the commission rate and flip status to Live.

2

Candidate Application & Auto-Assignment

Candidate applies on the public job board → System automatically assigns the candidate to an internal recruiter so screening starts immediately.

3

Shortlisting & Masked Resume Sharing

Internal recruiter shortlists candidate → Client HR receives a branded RS Bridge Resume displaying skills & experience, but NO phone number or email.

4

Digital Terms Signing & Interview Scheduling

Client HR clicks to interview candidate → Portal verifies if Terms of Business are signed → HR signs digitally (checkbox + typed legal name) → Candidate contact info unlocks!

5

Candidate Joining & Auto-Invoicing

Candidate joins the company → Recruiter marks status as "Joined" → System automatically drafts the placement commission invoice for founder review.

3. Candidate Privacy & Anti-Disintermediation Rules

Protection Mechanism How It Works Business Value to RS Bridge
Pre-Interview Resume Masking Candidate phone numbers and emails are stripped from candidate profiles prior to interview stage. Prevents companies from contacting candidates directly and skipping RS Bridge commission.
Digital Contract Gate Real contact info is unlocked only after HR accepts RS Bridge Terms of Business. Legally binds the company to pay commission on any candidate introduced by your firm.
Confidential Job Listings Public listings hide the hiring company's name (displayed as "Confidential Client"). Prevents job seekers from bypassing RS Bridge to apply directly on the company website.
Staff Financial Masking Internal recruiters see candidate pipeline status but cannot view commission rates or invoice totals. Keeps financial margins and total revenue completely private to the 2 founders.

4. Commercial Terms & Replacement Guarantee Rules

Commercial Rule Standard Provision System Enforcement
Commission Rates 8.33% to 25% of Annual CTC Snapshotted at placement creation; protected from retroactive edits.
Payment Terms 30 to 45 Days from candidate joining Calculates due date automatically; daily background check alerts founders if overdue.
Free Replacement Guarantee 60 to 90 Days replacement period Client submits resignation proof → Founders verify → Requirement re-opens for replacement hire.
Replacement Invoicing 1 Free replacement per placement Placements marked as replacement hires are automatically excluded from invoicing.
